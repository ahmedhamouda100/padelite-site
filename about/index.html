About us

Padel tournaments, organized with intent.

Padelite is a padel-tournament operator based in Egypt. We design, run and score competitive events for players at every level — from Americanos and mixed nights up to open-level knockouts and multi-week leagues.

Our mission

Give padel in Egypt the tournament infrastructure it deserves: a fair, algorithmic draw; live and transparent scoring; and a season-long ranking that rewards consistency, not just a single lucky Sunday.

How we work

Every Padelite event follows the same three commitments:

  • Fair draws. Snake-draft groups balanced by level and gender. The algorithm shows its work — there's no room for "why did I get put with them?" arguments.
  • Live scoring. Every match is scored in real time inside the app. Players, spectators and organizers watch the standings shift as points land. No paper, no confusion.
  • A ranking that matters. Every completed tournament credits points to your Padel Elite balance. Play consistently, climb the seasonal leaderboard, earn your spot at the closing event.

Who we work with

We partner with padel clubs, venue owners, and brands who want to bring high-quality competitive events to their courts and audiences. If that sounds like you, meet the sponsorship hub.
