How we build sponsorship.

We tailor each partnership to the brand — but here's the structure we start from. Pricing is quoted per conversation once we understand your goals.

Single event

Tournament partner

Your brand attached to one tournament — cover graphic, group-draw graphic, live score cards, podium cards, on-site logo on the trophy.

  • Tournament cover branding
  • Digital share assets
  • On-court signage (optional)
Recommended

Season partner

Continuous presence across every Padelite event in a season. Best value — cost per event drops significantly with volume.

  • Everything in Tournament partner, ×N events
  • Persistent placement on the ranking pages
  • Season opening + closing event feature
  • Content bundle at end of season
Custom

Category exclusivity

Full-category ownership across a season — apparel, beverage, telecom, financial services etc. — with a first-refusal on adjacent categories.

  • Category exclusivity in the season
  • Product placement in champion prize kits
  • Custom activation on match day
