Why Padelite works for sponsors

Padel players are one of the most naturally-shareable audiences in sport. They post their groups, their scores, their podium moments, and the shirts they wore doing it. Every Padelite event generates a stream of user-generated content — brand-safe, real, from people your target audience already trusts.

Sponsor a tournament and your brand appears alongside every one of those moments: the group draw graphic, the score share cards, the podium photos, the champion cards.
