Sponsorship · Audience

Who plays Padelite.

Padel is one of the fastest-growing sports in Egypt. The Padelite community sits at the competitive-social intersection — the players who post their groups, share their scores, and take their podium seriously.

Player profile

Our regulars are urban professionals in their 20s to 40s, with roughly balanced gender participation across mixed and open events. Levels range from 1.0 (new players) up to open competitive — organizers curate events by level so every audience is well-defined.

Engagement patterns

  • Weekly attendance. Players show up for multiple tournaments a month, not one-off events.
  • Native content. Every podium generates share cards that players post to Instagram, WhatsApp status and Stories.
  • In-app dwell. Players open the app to check schedules, live scores, standings and their Padel Elite ranking throughout the week — not just on match day.

Where your brand shows up

  • Group-draw graphics posted the moment groups are announced.
  • Score share cards after live matches.
  • Champion cards after every podium.
  • Tournament cover images on the app's home feed.
  • Optional on-court signage, shirts and prize sleeves.
