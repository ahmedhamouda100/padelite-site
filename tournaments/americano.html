Open Americano

The classic. Show up alone, rotate partners across rounds, score individual points. Perfect for meeting new players and getting a lot of court time in one evening.

Duration
~2–3 hours
Field
8–16 players
Partner
Not required
Ranking
Individual points

How it works

Players are seeded once, then Padelite generates a canonical ring rotation so nobody plays with the same partner twice — or against the same pair — until the ring wraps.

Scoring

Every match uses a fixed-point pool per round (typically 16 points total per match). Both teams' scores must sum to the round target. Ties are allowed — if a match ends 8-8, each player banks 8 points into their individual balance.

Standings

The leaderboard is gender-split (Men / Ladies), sorted by total points then point differential. Medals go to the top three of each pool at the end of the last round.

Who it's for

  • Players who want a lot of court time in one evening.
  • Newcomers who want to meet the regulars.
  • Any level — the ring rotation handles level mixing gracefully.

How to register

Get the Padelite app, find the Americano on your calendar, and tap Apply. If a payment receipt is required for that event, upload it inside the app for organizer verification.
