League

A season, not a day. Round-robin across all registered teams, played over multiple weeks, with an optional Grand Final between the top two.

Duration
4–8 weeks
Field
6–12 teams
Format
Round-robin
Optional
Grand Final

How the season runs

At the start of the league, Padelite generates the full fixture list — every team is scheduled against every other team, spread across the weeks you choose. Fixtures can be rescheduled per-match if a team needs to move a game.

Standings

The live table doubles as your standings all season long. It's ordered by points, then game differential. When the final week finishes, positions lock and the podium is announced.

Optional Grand Final

Enable a Grand Final when you create the league, and the top two teams meet one more time at the end of the season for the trophy. Whichever team wins the Grand Final takes the title regardless of regular-season standings.

Padel Elite points

League placements credit points into your Padel Elite balance the same way tournament finishes do. Weekly consistency compounds.
