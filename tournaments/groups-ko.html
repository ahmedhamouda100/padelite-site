Groups + Knockout

The tournament format. Register with a partner; top teams from each group advance to a knockout bracket. Cross-group seeding means no boring rematches in the quarter-finals.

Duration
Full day
Field
8–32 teams
Structure
Groups + KO
Optional
Upper / lower bracket

Group stage

Teams are drawn into balanced groups. Every team plays every other team in their group; the top N advance based on wins, then game differential, then head-to-head.

Bracket phase

Padelite auto-seeds the knockout — group winners cross with runners-up from other groups, avoiding same-group rematches until at least the semi-finals. Manual seeding is also available if the organizer wants to shape the draw.

Third-place playoff

Semi-final losers meet in a third-place match — auto-seeded — so nobody leaves without a shot at the podium.

Upper / lower brackets

For bigger events, we run parallel Upper and Lower brackets so early-round losers stay in the tournament. The final placement respects both sides of the bracket.

How to register

Get the Padelite app, find the tournament on your calendar, apply with your partner. If your partner is a Padelite player, they'll get a notification to confirm.
