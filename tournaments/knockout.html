Seeding options

Padelite supports three seeding modes: fully automatic (by team level average), manual pair-based seeding for shaping the draw, and hybrid — you pin the top seeds and let the algorithm fill the rest.

Bracket math

The bracket generator handles all standard sizes (R32, R16, QF, SF, Final) with proper first-round byes. There's no manual math to worry about; the app draws it, schedules it, and updates it as scores land.

Live scoring & reschedule

Every match is scored live. Long-press any match to open the reschedule sheet — swap courts, shift start time per-match or bulk-shift from a given match onwards.

Third-place playoff

Semi-final losers meet in a third-place match, so both bronze medals are earned on court, not by disqualification.
