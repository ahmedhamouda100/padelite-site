Padel Elite · Rules

How the points work.

The exact points structure Padelite uses to compute your Padel Elite balance. Clear rules, no surprises.

Registration bonus

Every player who registers, plays, and completes a tournament earns +2 points the moment the event is finalized. Consistency matters.

Podium bonuses

Podium finishers earn extra points on top of the registration bonus:

  • 1st place — 10 points
  • 2nd place — 8 points
  • 3rd place — 6 points
  • 4th place — 4 points

Individual formats (Americanos) award podium points to individual players; team formats award them to both members of the team.

Tie-breakers

When the seasonal leaderboard has ties, we break them in this order:

  1. Total Padel Elite points
  2. Number of 1st-place finishes
  3. Number of podium finishes (top 4)
  4. Number of tournaments played (rewards showing up)
  5. Head-to-head record between the tied players, where available

Gender-scoped leaderboards

The seasonal ranking is presented in two pools — Men and Ladies — reflecting how competitive results play out in mixed and gender-split events alike.

Season boundaries

A Padel Elite season is closed when the community's closing tournament of that period is finalized. Balances roll over into the next season, but new-season leaderboards focus on points earned within the season window.
