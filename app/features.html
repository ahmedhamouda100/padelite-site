The app · Features

Every feature, one screen away.

Padelite runs every format we've seen on court — plus the parts organizers used to do by hand.

Register in seconds

Apply solo or with a partner, pay from the app, get a live status the moment the organizer approves.

Snake-draft groups

Balanced groups by level and gender in one tap. Move players around after the draw with drag-and-drop.

Active court queue

See every court in play, pick the next match by rest time, and prevent double-booking automatically.

Live standings

Standings update the instant a score is saved. Group filter, gender split, head-to-head tie-breaker.

Padel Elite points

Every completed tournament credits points to your Padel Elite balance. Climb the seasonal leaderboard.

Share the podium

Auto-generated Hall of Fame cards for winners. One tap to share to Instagram, WhatsApp or Stories.
