The app

Everything Padelite, in your pocket.

Every Padelite tournament runs on our own app — Padelite for iOS and Android. Players use it to browse the calendar, register, pay, get their match schedule, and watch live standings. Organizers use it to draw groups, dispatch courts, score matches, and finalize the podium.
