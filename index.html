Who we are

Padelite is a padel-tournament operator based in Egypt. We design, run and score competitive events for players at every level — from Americanos and mixed nights up to open-level knockouts and multi-week leagues.

Our process is built on three commitments: a fair, algorithmic draw; live and transparent scoring; and a season-long ranking that rewards consistency, not just a single lucky Sunday.

We work with clubs, venues and brands to deliver tournaments players actually talk about on Monday — with the operational rigor of a professional league and the atmosphere of a home court.

What we do

Padel needs more than a court and a WhatsApp group. It needs a proper process. Here's what we handle for every event.

Weekly tournaments

Multiple events every week across Egypt — from level-based Americanos to open Mixed nights. There's always something to play.

Fair draws

Snake-drafted groups that respect level and gender balance. No more "how did I get put with them" arguments — the algorithm shows its work.

Live scoring

Every match is scored in real time. Players and spectators watch standings shift as points land. No paper, no confusion.

Padel Elite ranking

Every tournament credits points to your Padel Elite balance. Play consistently, climb the seasonal leaderboard, earn your spot at the closing event.

A real community

The same players show up week after week. Hall of Fame, share-worthy podium cards, level history — the moments that matter, saved.

Predictable calendar

Weekly slots, published in advance, with clear entry fees and levels. No last-minute changes — plan your padel around it.

Tournament formats

Every event we run is one of these. Pick a format that matches your level, your schedule, and how competitive you want to be.

Individual · social

Open Americano

The classic. Show up alone, rotate partners across rounds, score individual points. Perfect for meeting new players and getting a lot of court time in one evening.

  • ~2–3 hours total
  • No partner needed
  • Fixed-point scoring — every point counts
  • Gender-split leaderboard
Individual · competitive

Grouped Americano

Bigger field, split into balanced groups by level and gender. Round-robin inside your group, then a Finals stage for the top qualifiers. The most competitive individual format we run.

  • Group stage + Finals
  • Snake-draft groups with gender quotas
  • Podium points (10 / 8 / 6 / 4)
  • Half or full-day event
Team · flagship

Groups + Knockout

The tournament format. Register with a partner, top teams from each group advance to a knockout bracket. Cross-group seeding means no boring rematches in the quarter-finals.

  • Group stage + full bracket
  • Upper / lower brackets available
  • Third-place playoff seeded automatically
  • Champion card + trophy
Team · short format

Knockout only

Single-elimination from R32 all the way to the Final. High-stakes matches from the first ball. For teams who want a fast, decisive event.

  • Half-day format
  • Manual or hybrid seeding
  • Straight to the podium
Team · long format

League

A season, not a day. Round-robin across all registered teams, played over multiple weeks, with an optional Grand Final between the top two.

  • Runs over 4–8 weeks
  • Every team plays every team
  • Optional Grand Final
  • Season points feed Padel Elite

Everything, in one app.

Every Padelite tournament runs on our own app — Padelite for iOS and Android. Players use it to see the calendar, register, pay, get their match schedule, and watch live standings. Organizers use it to draw groups, dispatch courts, score matches, and finalize the podium.

The app is free. You don't need it to play — you can always register with us directly — but once you start using it, you won't go back to WhatsApp brackets.
